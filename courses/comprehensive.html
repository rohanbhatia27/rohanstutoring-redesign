Between classes

Use the feedback before you try another question.

For S1, return to a question you missed and find the wording in the passage that supports the answer. Write down why your original choice was less convincing.

For S2, revise a paragraph after reading your marking notes. Check whether the evidence supports your claim and whether you have explained how the conclusion follows. Apply that change when you write your next essay.

Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling.

What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded and shared after the session. You won't fall behind if something comes up. But attending live lets you ask questions and get coaching that recordings can't replicate.

Does this course cover Section 3?

No. Comprehensive focuses on Section 1 reasoning and Section 2 writing. If you need Section 3 preparation, plan that separately alongside this course.

How many students are in the cohort?

The cohort is capped at 20 students so there is room for questions and discussion during live classes.

Is a private onboarding call included?

No. Comprehensive includes group classes, five marked essays, the recorded library, direct access to Rohan and fortnightly open Q&A. Private onboarding is part of the Mastery Program.

Do I need to watch all 80+ hours?

No. The library is a reference for your S1 and S2 practice. Start with the topic you are working on, practise it, and use the relevant recording when you need another explanation or worked example.

How long is each class, and when do they run?

Each session is 1 hour. The first classes are Sunday 29 November (S1) and Tuesday 1 December (S2). S1 runs Sundays at 6pm and S2 runs Tuesdays at 6pm, Sydney/Melbourne time. In the UK and Ireland that's 7am Sunday and 7am Tuesday. There are no classes from 20 December to 9 January, a three-week Christmas and New Year break. The last classes before it are Sunday 13 December (S1) and Tuesday 15 December (S2). S1 resumes on 10 January and S2 on 12 January. S1 pauses after 31 January so S2 can finish before the Section 2 sitting. S2 also meets on Sunday 7 February and finishes on Tuesday 16 February. S1 returns on Sunday 7 March, with an extra S1 class on Tuesday 9 March, and finishes on Sunday 14 March. Every session is recorded if you cannot make one live.

Are the live classes recorded?

Yes. Every class is recorded and shared via Google Drive shortly after the session ends. You can revisit any class at any time throughout the program. That said, attending live gives you something recordings can't replicate: the ability to ask questions, get real-time feedback, and leave each session with something concrete.

When do I get access to the recorded library?

Immediately upon enrolment. You'll receive the same recorded library included in the Blueprint, shared via Google Drive before the first live class begins.

Is this only for science students?

Most students here come from a science background: Biomedicine, Nursing, Physio, Pharmacy and similar. Plenty of students without one have done well, because the course teaches reasoning and writing from the ground up. If you are coming from a non-science background, book a free strategy consultation and I will tell you honestly whether this cohort or the Blueprint is the better fit.

How does the refund guarantee work?

Try the first two scheduled classes live or by recording. If the course isn't right for you, email me before your third class and we'll refund you in full. Using the included library does not affect this guarantee. On an approved full refund, we refund course payments already made and cancel all future course instalments. And if you enrol, get your September results back and you've done well enough that you don't need to sit again in March, email me and I'll refund you in full too.

Can I still do this if I work shifts or live in a different time zone?

Yes. We already have students across Australia, the UK and Ireland, and the course still works if your live attendance is imperfect. Every class is recorded, so you can keep up with the weekly structure, homework, question banks, and essays on your own schedule. If you want a standing monthly check-in and private tutorials on top of the live classes, that is what the Mastery Program adds.
