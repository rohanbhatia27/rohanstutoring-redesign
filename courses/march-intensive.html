Questions

Before you enrol.

Can I do this while working full-time or on placement?

Yes. That's why it's on weekends. Two one-hour classes a weekend, plus that week's Game Plan. If a shift clashes, watch the recording.

What times are the classes outside Sydney?

S1 is Saturdays 9 to 10am Sydney/Melbourne time (8am Brisbane, 6am Perth, 10pm Friday in the UK and Ireland). S2 is Sundays 7 to 8pm Sydney/Melbourne time (6pm Brisbane, 4pm Perth, 8am Sunday in the UK and Ireland). Every class is recorded.

Who marks my essays?

Rohan does. Every one. Your baseline essay is due Wednesday 6 January and comes back by 13 January. You submit two more across January, and the timed mock is due Wednesday 10 February and comes back by 17 February, before the live debrief.

What happens over Christmas?

No classes from 21 December to 9 January. You get the No-Backslide Holiday Plan and write your baseline essay. On Sunday 10 January, the Restart Session gets everyone moving again.

Why does S1 stop in February?

Because S2 is sat first, on 27 and 28 February. S1 and S3 come later, from 19 March. So February is all S2, with the Keep-Warm Clinic on 13 February to keep your S1 sharp. Then S1 comes back for the two weeks before its sitting.

Is it only for re-sitters?

No. First-timers get stuck on the last two options too. Your baseline essay shows Rohan where your writing starts, whatever your history.

How do the instalments work?

$629 a month for 3 months ($959 for Premium). The first payment is when you enrol. Paying in full costs less, and on the standard course Rohan marks a fifth essay.

How does the refund guarantee work?

Come to or watch the first two classes on 12 and 13 December. Not for you? Email before the third class on 19 December. You get a full refund and any unpaid instalments are cancelled. Your Blueprint access ends when the refund goes through.
