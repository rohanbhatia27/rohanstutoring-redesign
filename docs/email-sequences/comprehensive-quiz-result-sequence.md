## Email 4: Why self-study is often not enough

Preview: Sometimes all you need is a little bit of guidance...

Hi {{ subscriber.first_name | default: "there" }},

Self-study is not bad.

Plenty of students can use a recorded course, stay disciplined, review properly, and improve without live classes.

The fact that the Study Plan quiz suggested the Comprehensive Pathway to you, is because you would benefit from more structure than a self-paced library can give you.

Not because you are incapable. But because the exam is too strange, too long, and too easy to misread when you are doing everything alone.

The danger with self-study is that it lets you avoid the work you most need.

- If essays feel uncomfortable, you watch another S1 video. But that doesn't fix the core issue, does it?
- If timed sections expose a weakness, you go back to untimed practice. But then you run out of time in the exam.
- If a mock goes badly, you call it a bad day and move on. Then you stress you didn't practice enough...

Working with a tutor is not *magic*. It is just a better route to keep you accountable and have someone in your corner as you battle this exam.
